About

The approach behind the work.

I'm a graphic, motion, and UI/UX designer based in Lithuania, working with brands across the US, musicians, labels and YouTubers. Six years of hands-on work in Figma, Photoshop, After Effects, and Sony Vegas show up in everything from UI/UX design to cover art, thumbnails, posters, and animated logos.

I have a genuine passion for this work, and I love collaborating on bigger projects that call for close communication and teamwork. That said, I'm just as comfortable handling a project solo from start to finish - either way, my main goal is making sure the client gets exactly what they're looking for.

Expect quick turnarounds and direct communication over a long back-and-forth. Most projects start from a rough reference or a one-line brief and get refined together from there - and if something isn't working, I'll say so and fix it rather than just deliver what was asked for.

Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026. This policy explains what personal information Artiszt collects through this site, how it's used, and the rights you have. Any changes will be posted here with a new date.

What's collected

Through the contact form: your name, email address, project type, and message. Sending it is up to you, but without a name and email address there's no way to reply. No account is created and no payment details are collected on this site.

GitHub, which hosts the site, records visitors' IP addresses for security, under its own privacy statement. When you start filling in the contact form, your browser also asks Google for a one-time code that keeps out automated spam, so Google sees your IP address at that point; the code itself identifies no one.

How it's used

Your enquiry is used only to reply to you and, if a project goes ahead, to agree and deliver it. This is based on the consent you give when you send the form and, once a project is agreed, on the contract for it. The form's spam check relies on a legitimate interest: keeping the site secure and working. Your information is never sold, used for advertising, or used for profiling or automated decisions.

How long it's kept

Enquiries that don't lead to a project are deleted within a year of the last message. For projects, emails and records are kept for as long as Lithuanian tax and accounting rules require. The spam check keeps nothing about you: its codes expire within six hours. GitHub keeps its records for as long as its privacy statement says.

Who handles it

Two services handle this information:

  • GitHub hosts the website and records visitors' IP addresses for security.
  • Google runs the form's spam check and hosts the inbox (Gmail), and puts each enquiry in it through a script in Artiszt's own Google account.

Both may process data outside the EU/EEA, including in the United States. Both are certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission recognises as providing adequate protection.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses no analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies, and no other company uses it to follow your activity across websites. Because nothing tracks you, the site works the same whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal. The Cookie Policy explains the two small notes it keeps in your browser.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask to see, correct, or delete your information, to restrict its use, or to get a copy to take elsewhere. You can withdraw your consent at any time; this doesn't affect anything done before. To do any of this, email the address below. You can also complain to a data protection authority.

Right to object: you can object at any time to the form's spam check, which relies on a legitimate interest. GitHub's records of visits are covered by GitHub's privacy statement.
